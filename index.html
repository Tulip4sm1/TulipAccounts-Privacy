Privacy Policy

TulipAccounts
Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

TulipAccounts is an offline personal finance application. All of your financial information is stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

TulipAccounts does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal information. All of your financial data remains on your device.

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No financial information is uploaded.
  • No account information is shared.
  • No internet connection is required to use the app.

Permissions

Storage Access (Storage Access Framework)

This permission is used only when you choose to export or import backup files. TulipAccounts accesses only the files or folders that you explicitly select.

Security

  • Your financial data remains on your device.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose to export them.
  • No cloud synchronization is performed.
  • Your information is never transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

TulipAccounts does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party services that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

TulipAccounts is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date.
